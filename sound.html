Weather sounds

The wind and the rain as the game makes them (src/audio/ambience.js), made from noise as they play, and the birds (src/audio/birds.js), sung from two recordings in a new order each time. Pick a weather: it goes over to it in a few seconds, the way the game blends one into the next. In the game the same sound follows the weather on its own; cmd.weather('storm') in the console jumps to one.
