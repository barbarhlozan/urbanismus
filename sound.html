Sounds

The birds (src/audio/birds.js), sung from two recordings in a new order each time, as many as the weather and the hour bring, and the rain and the wind in the woods (src/audio/bed.js). Below, the pencil and the taps (src/audio/effects.js): each press is another one. Pick a weather: it goes over to it in a few seconds, the way the game blends one into the next. In the game the same sound follows the weather on its own; cmd.weather('storm') in the console jumps to one.
