# What everything that can be built is called, and its few words in the
# Build menu. How to write it: text/README.md. Read by src/ui/names.js.
#
# The key is the thing's name as story/unlocks.txt calls it (narodni_vybor,
# deep_mine); <name>.blurb its few words, shared by its sizes, or <id>.blurb
# for one size of it. The names are those used in Czechoslovakia between
# the 1950s and the 1980s; the English follows the Czech as closely as it
# can, and leaves the names of the time's own institutions as they were
# (JZD, Jednota, Tuzex, VB, ČSAD).
