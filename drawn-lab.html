Drawn houses

A test page, apart from the game: a building's own faces painted by hand on a blueprint, shown as the map and a photo would show them.

Painted

The building is the game's own (); its blueprint lays every face out flat at its true shape. Download it and import it into Procreate as the bottom layer, paint the faces on layers above it, square the windows in magenta, hide the blueprint layer and export a PNG. Paint outside the outlines is left out.
