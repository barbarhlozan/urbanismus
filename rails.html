Railway track sketches

Close-zoom treatments for the railway, drawn with the game's own code: the isometric camera (tile 34), the roads' sway (wobble in render/painter.js), the station (structures/station.js) through the Painter with its shadows, the M 152.0 railcar (render/vehicles.js) and the level-crossing signs (render/crossings.js). Line widths are the map's pens in device pixels, as the game draws them at each zoom. Nothing in the game has changed.
